TEAM PHOTOS — put member photos in this folder.

Photo specs:
- Square image (1:1), minimum 600 x 600 pixels (bigger is fine)
- Format: .jpg or .png
- File name: the member's slug, e.g. syed-bukhari.jpg, ammar-abdullah.jpg,
  shoaib-rasool.jpg, arqam.jpg
- A plain, professional head-and-shoulders photo works best; the card
  crops the image to a square automatically.

After adding a photo:
1. Open app/data/team.js
2. Find the member and set their photo, e.g.
     photo: "/team/syed-bukhari.jpg",
3. Rebuild / redeploy the site. Until a photo is set, the member's card
   shows an initials avatar in the brand colours.
